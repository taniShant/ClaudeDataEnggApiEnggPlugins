## Areas to Cover

### Visual Identity
- Colour palette — primary, secondary, and semantic colours (approved values, no arbitrary hex)
- Typography — typeface, scale, weight conventions for digital screens
- Iconography — approved icon sets, sizing, usage rules
- Spacing and layout grid conventions
- Logo usage rules — clearspace, colour variants, prohibited uses

### Tone of Voice
- Brand personality — the Barclays voice (approachable, clear, confident)
- Writing style — active voice, sentence length, plain English principles
- Prohibited language — jargon, technical terms to avoid, competitor references
- Regulatory language — required FCA/PRA disclosure phrases and how to integrate naturally
- Channel-specific tone variations (app vs. web vs. SMS vs. email)

### Copy Conventions
- Headlines — character limits, capitalisation style
- CTAs — approved verb patterns (e.g. "Continue", "Confirm", "Apply now")
- Error messages — empathetic, non-blaming, action-oriented
- Notifications — push, SMS, in-app banner conventions
- Microcopy — field labels, hints, placeholders
