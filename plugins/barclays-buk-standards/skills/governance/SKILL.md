## Known Governance Processes (to be documented)

| Process | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **DRA** (Delivered Risk Assessment) | Post-delivery risk sign-off |
| **FRA** (Forward Risk Assessment) | Pre-delivery risk assessment |
| **Change Control** | Managing changes to baseline scope, cost, or timeline |
| **RAIDs** | Risks, Assumptions, Issues, Dependencies log |
| **BUK MCF** (Mission Critical Forum) | Escalation forum for mission-critical decisions |
| **Group MCF** | Group-level equivalent of BUK MCF |
| **NAPA** | New and Pipeline Approval — new product governance (see `consumer-duty` skill for CDA requirements) |
| **Business Impact Assessment** | Assessing business impact of changes |
| **BUK Capacity Planning** | Resource and capacity forecasting |
| **Investment Planning Hub** | Investment proposals and funding approval |
| **PE Management (inc DCP)** | Programme/project economics and delivery change process |
| **Proposals and approval to baseline** | Formal approval to proceed and set delivery baseline |
| **PMO & Controls Maturity** | Delivery governance health and maturity assessment |
| **Mandatory, Regulatory & Control Delivery** | Tracking mandatory and regulatory change delivery |
| **Lessons Learnt** | Capturing and sharing delivery learnings |
| **Stand Up of Stand Up & EP Delivery Forum** | Delivery status reporting forums |
| **PMO Community of Practice** | PMO standards, tooling, and practice sharing |
| **BUK Compliance Dashboard** | Compliance status visibility across BUK |
